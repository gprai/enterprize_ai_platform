W2-01 — RETRIEVAL MODELS
========================

BUSINESS PURPOSE
The retrieval models give document chunks, requests, authorization context,
collection specifications, and search results one validated meaning across
all vector-store adapters. This reduces the risk that different backends
interpret tenant, classification, identity, or embedding information
differently.

ARCHITECTURE POSITION
The models are the data boundary above the deterministic fake, Qdrant, and
OpenSearch adapters. They express the owner-approved DATA-002, DATA-003,
EMB-001, and SEC-001 contracts without depending on a vendor client.

TECHNICAL IMPORTANCE
- Deterministic identifiers make repeated ingestion of the same approved
  identity inputs address the same logical chunk.
- Validated metadata gives authorization and adapters a stable contract.
- A single approved embedding dimension prevents incompatible vectors from
  being silently mixed.
- Document version, content hash, owner, PII status, and ingestion status
  preserve provenance and lifecycle meaning.

LAYMAN EXPLANATION
These models are the standard form attached to every searchable page. Every
database receives the same fields and rules, so changing the filing system
does not change what the page means or who may see it.
