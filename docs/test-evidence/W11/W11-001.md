## Exact governed SLI contracts

All 17 contracts use a 30-day window. The denominator is the named eligible
population, not an arbitrary count. `minimum_samples` must be met before a
measured value may decide PASS or FAIL; otherwise the result is
`INDETERMINATE`. Each data source below is a configured source reference, not
proof that live samples reached it. The authoritative fields and identities
are in `configs/observability/slo.yaml`.

| SLI | Population and denominator | Minimum samples | Target | Data source | Policy |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Availability | eligible AI requests | 100 | at least 0.999 | `enterprise_ai_stage_executions_total` | paired burn |
| API latency p95 | successful API requests | 100 | at most 4 seconds | `enterprise_ai_stage_duration_seconds_bucket` | paired burn |
| API latency p99 | successful API requests | 100 | at most 10 seconds | `enterprise_ai_stage_duration_seconds_bucket` | paired burn |
| Retrieval latency p99 | completed retrieval requests | 100 | at most 0.75 seconds | `enterprise_ai_stage_duration_seconds_bucket` | paired burn |
| Answer correctness | answered evaluation cases | 20 | at least 0.95 | `evaluation.report.answered_correctness` | release gate |
| Grounded-answer rate | answered AI requests | 20 | at least 0.95 | `enterprise_ai_ai_groundedness` | release gate |
| Citation coverage | citation-required answers | 20 | at least 1.0 | `enterprise_ai_ai_citation_coverage` | release gate |
| Retrieval relevance | retrieval evaluation cases | 20 | at least 0.85 | `enterprise_ai_ai_retrieval_quality` | release gate |
| Correct abstention rate | expected-abstention cases | 20 | at least 0.95 | `evaluation.report.abstention_correctness` | release gate |
| Unauthorized retrieval rate | authorization attempts | 1 | at most 0 | `enterprise_ai_stage_executions_total` | immediate |
| Safety violation rate | safety evaluation cases | 1 | at most 0 | `enterprise_ai_safety_violations_total` | immediate |
| PII leakage rate | PII evaluation cases | 1 | at most 0 | `evaluation.rag_safety.pii_failures` | immediate |
| Ingestion freshness | governed ingestion runs | 1 | at most 24 hours | `governed_ingestion_manifest.completed_at` | release gate |
| Retrieval dependency availability | retrieval dependency attempts | 100 | at least 0.999 | `enterprise_ai_ai_dependency_availability` | paired burn |
| Model dependency availability | model dependency attempts | 100 | at least 0.999 | `enterprise_ai_ai_dependency_availability` | paired burn |
| Tokens per successful query | successful AI queries | 20 | at most 16,896 tokens | `enterprise_ai_usage_tokens_total` | release gate |
| Cost per successful query | successful AI queries | 20 | at most USD 0.01 | `enterprise_ai_ai_request_cost_usd_bucket` | release gate |

For minimum objectives, the remaining ratio budget is derived from the
allowed bad fraction (`1 - threshold`) and observed bad fraction (`1 -
observed`). For maximum ratio objectives, it uses threshold as the allowed
bad fraction. The result is bounded to `[0, 1]`; non-ratio SLOs do not invent
an error-budget ratio. An absent or weak observation is never represented as
a healthy zero.

Fast availability burn requires both 5-minute and 1-hour factors to reach
14.4 with at least 20 short-window events; slow burn requires both 30-minute
and 6-hour factors to reach 6.0 with at least 20 short-window events. One
breached window alone does not establish a paired breach. Missing measurement
returns `NOT RUN`; insufficient or unstable evidence returns `INDETERMINATE`.
The Prometheus availability rules have corresponding critical/warning owners.

`FAIL` takes precedence and freezes release for **review** of rollback; it
does not roll back automatically. Missing/extra SLI identities or missing burn
tiers yield `INDETERMINATE`. `INDETERMINATE` and `NOT RUN` both block release;
only complete PASS evidence permits it. One-off UTC maintenance is capped at
120 minutes, disallows standing exclusions, and cannot suppress
unauthorized-retrieval, safety or PII SLIs. Critical escalation targets the
primary, backup and task owner at 5, 10 and 15 minutes; warning targets are
30 and 60 minutes. These are contracts, not evidence of delivery or response.
