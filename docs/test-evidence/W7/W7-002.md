# W7-002 — Deterministic Processing, Quality and Safe Replay Evidence

## Scope and business outcome

W7-002 extends the governed W7-001 ingestion handoff with deterministic,
provider-neutral processing and quality controls. Its business purpose is to
make each approved batch reproducible, inspectable and safe to replay without
silently duplicating embeddings or expanding tenant access.

The implemented local flow is:

`immutable W7-001 handoff → bounded replay-source selection → deterministic extraction and normalization → schema and quality checks → quarantine, dead letter or logical VALIDATED/CURATED state → existing embedding/index handoff`

In plain language, this is a factory line where every batch has a recipe, an
inspection station and a traceable reject bin. W7-001 owns secure intake,
physical storage zones, lineage, identity, quarantine foundations and the
embedding-eligibility handoff. W7-002 owns transformation, schema and quality
enforcement, drift evaluation, checkpoint/resume behavior and logical
`VALIDATED`/`CURATED` processing states after that handoff. It does not rename
or create physical storage prefixes.

## Processing and quality contract

The strict configuration fixes the following boundaries:

- six ordered required fields: `document_id`, `tenant_id`, `classification`,
  `source_identity_sha256`, `content_sha256` and
  `normalized_content_sha256`;
- minimum drift sample of 20 documents;
- volume PASS from 50% through 200% of the configured expected count,
  inclusive, and FAIL outside that range;
- duplicate-rate PASS at or below 5%, WARNING above 5% through 10%, and FAIL
  above 10%; batches below 20 documents report `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` rather
  than duplicate-rate PASS;
- identity/content conflict always fails and quarantines independently of the
  percentage;
- two attempts, a 120-second per-document deadline and sequential concurrency
  of one;
- replay limited to 100 documents, 5 MiB per document, 100 MiB total, 15
  minutes, a maximum source age of 24 hours, one tenant and immutable versions;
- replay policy identity must equal the loaded configuration identity; and
- destructive reprocessing remains disallowed by the request schema.

Replay source and W7-001 `EmbeddingEligibility` identities are bound before
processing. Tenant, classification, source identity and content identity must
agree. The extractor accepts only approved text, Markdown and PDF contracts,
verifies byte count and content hash, applies deterministic normalization and
records only safe reason codes and hashes in evidence.

Logical state transitions are distinct from W7-001 physical prefixes:

- `VALIDATED` means a dry-run candidate passed extraction and quality checks;
- `CURATED` means a non-dry-run candidate passed and reached the existing
  embedding/index handoff;
- `QUARANTINED` records fail-closed schema, quality, identity, content or PII
  outcomes;
- `DEAD_LETTERED` records exhausted retryable extraction, embedding or indexing
  failures;
- `SKIPPED_DUPLICATE` prevents a second embedding for exact idempotent replay;
  and
- a terminal checkpoint prevents resolved documents from being processed
  again during resume.

Quality reports have deterministic identities, threshold identities, exact
counts/rates, redacted observations and failure metrics for extraction, PII
detection, embedding, indexing, schema drift, volume drift and duplicates.
They contain no source content or plaintext secrets.
