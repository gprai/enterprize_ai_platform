WHAT THE GATE PROTECTS
----------------------

The W5 gate checks whether supported sandbox infrastructure changes preserve
the owner's approved Region, tags, resource lifetime, encryption, private
networking, IAM scope, S3, KMS, OpenSearch, ECR, secret, and exception controls.
It fails closed when input is incomplete, malformed, unsupported, unknown, or
not evaluated against the complete ordered policy inventory.

WHY TWO ENGINES ARE REQUIRED
----------------------------

OPA applies the project-specific decision contract to strict Terraform
plan-shaped input. Checkov independently scans Terraform source with built-in
checks under an offline configuration. Both must pass; partial execution or
disagreement is not PASS. This separation helps detect both project-specific
decision violations and broader static infrastructure findings.

WHAT BLOCKS THE GATE
--------------------

The gate blocks for any effective OPA finding, Checkov failure, invalid or
unsupported input, missing policy context, unapproved Region or tag value,
unsafe lifecycle action, weak encryption/network/IAM control, secret material,
invalid exception data, tool disagreement, or incomplete execution.

HOW EXCEPTIONS ARE GOVERNED
---------------------------

The exception registry starts empty. Any later exception must be explicit,
resource-scoped, owner- and approver-bound, evidenced, and valid for no more
than seven days. It may name 1-10 exact Terraform resource addresses. Wildcard
or blanket exceptions are prohibited. W5-PLAN-001, W5-SEC-001, and W5-EXC-001
cannot be exempted. The 20 approved Checkov skips remain separate skips and are
not silently converted into OPA exceptions or PASS results.
