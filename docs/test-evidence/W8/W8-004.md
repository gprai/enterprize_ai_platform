## Business and architecture outcome

The implemented decision flow is:

`demand assumptions → strict capacity policy → aggregate synthetic sample → deterministic evaluator → PASS/FAIL reason codes → conditional engine decision`

This is like load-testing a bridge with controlled weights before deciding
whether a specialized lane is needed. The controlled weights can expose a
design defect, but they cannot prove how the bridge behaves under real traffic,
weather, operating practices, or long-term cost.

Bedrock remains selected for approved foundation-model inference. SageMaker
real-time remains conditional on a justified owner-controlled or fine-tuned
artifact. EKS model serving and Karpenter remain conditional on documented
workload or platform-control needs. Ray Serve is **NOT ADOPTED** because no
programmable multi-model requirement was measured. Triton is **NOT ADOPTED**
because no GPU batching or optimized-runtime requirement was measured. No
engine was installed, provisioned, or called.

`(provider token cost + allocated compute cost + observability/network cost) / successful requests`

## Interfaces, identities, and fail-closed behavior

- `load_capacity_policy(path=DEFAULT_POLICY_PATH) -> CapacityPolicy` reads a
  UTF-8 mapping no larger than 65,536 bytes, rejects duplicate keys, aliases,
  invalid encoding, and extra or mistyped fields.
- `policy_identity(policy) -> str` and `fingerprint(value) -> str` produce
  lowercase SHA-256 identities over canonical sorted compact UTF-8 JSON.
- `evaluate_capacity(policy, sample) -> CapacityEvidence` accepts aggregate
  synthetic measurements only and verifies policy identity, timestamp and
  pricing freshness, request accounting, successful population, percentile,
  throughput, failure, scaling, termination, and cost contracts.
- `CapacitySample` and `CapacityEvidence` are strict immutable Pydantic models.
  Evidence fixes `production_capacity_claimed` to `false` and
  `live_execution` to `NOT_AUTHORIZED`.
- Public reason codes contain no prompt, model output, tenant content,
  credential, provider envelope, endpoint, or exception detail.
