W2-08 — TENANT ISOLATION
========================

BUSINESS PURPOSE
Tenant isolation prevents one customer or organizational unit from receiving
another tenant's document chunks, even when those chunks are semantically
similar to the query.

ARCHITECTURE POSITION
Tenant identity is part of both the requester's authorization context and
the stored chunk metadata. Retrieval applies the tenant boundary together
with classification clearance and approved role or group rules before a
result may be returned.

OWNER-EXECUTED EVIDENCE
Result: PASS
Check: tests/security/test_tenant_isolation.py
Observed result: 4 passed.

The complete local suite later reported 157 passed, 1 expected skip, and
3 deselected. The Qdrant internal integration also passed using synthetic

TECHNICAL IMPORTANCE
- Similarity is not authorization.
- Filtering before return reduces the chance that unauthorized content
  reaches application memory, logs, prompts, or downstream models.
- A common contract keeps tenant behavior consistent across adapters.
- Fail-closed behavior prevents missing security context from becoming broad
  access.

LAYMAN EXPLANATION
The system searches only inside the requester's locked filing cabinet. It
does not search every customer's cabinet and hide the wrong pages afterward.

PRIVACY BOUNDARY
Tests used synthetic local content. No evidence shows processing of real
customer data, credentials, or sensitive business documents. A local PASS
does not establish production residency, retention, encryption, audit, or
compliance controls.

NOT RUN
- Live identity-provider or token validation.
- Live AWS IAM or OpenSearch domain authorization.
- Production penetration, privacy, or cross-region isolation testing.
- Real multi-tenant business data.

ROLLBACK AND FAILURE
The safe response to a missing tenant, tenant mismatch, insufficient
clearance, or unauthorized role/group is denial. Authorization must never
retry with a broader tenant filter. No source or deployment rollback was run;
local Qdrant teardown passed with zero project-labelled residue.
